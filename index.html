When the patient can't speak, every detail matters.

In an emergency, responders may not know who the patient is, what they are allergic to, what medication they take, or whom to contact.

PRIVACY BY DESIGN

Emergency access doesn't mean unlimited access.

The system separates critical emergency information from protected medical records. Different users receive different levels of access.

✓
Minimum necessary information Only critical information is shown to an emergency helper.
✓
Role-based access Medical professionals can access additional protected information.
✓
Access is logged Emergency access events are recorded for accountability.
✓
Temporary access Emergency access can automatically expire after a defined period.
