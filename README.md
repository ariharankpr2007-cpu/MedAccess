# 🏥 MedAccess

## Emergency Medical Access Platform

MedAccess is a web-based platform designed to provide
controlled access to essential medical information during
emergency situations.

The system focuses on making critical medical information
accessible when needed while maintaining controlled access
to protected patient information.

---

## 🚨 Key Features

- 🆔 Emergency medical identification
- 📱 QR-based medical access
- 🚨 Emergency information access
- 🔐 Role-based access
- 📋 Patient medical profiles
- 📝 Access logging
- 👤 Controlled information visibility
- 📱 Responsive web interface

---

## 🔐 Access Control

MedAccess separates information based on the level of access
required.

### Emergency Information

Essential information that may be required during an emergency.

Examples include:

- Blood group
- Allergies
- Critical medication information
- Emergency-related details

### Protected Information

More detailed patient information is kept behind controlled
access.

This approach aims to reduce unnecessary exposure of
sensitive medical information.
